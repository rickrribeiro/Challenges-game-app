# Usage Guide

## App Flow

### 1. Setup Screen
When you first launch the app, you'll see the Setup Screen where you:
- Enter the number of players (2-10)
- Select difficulty level:
  - **Easy** (Green) - 10 points per challenge
  - **Medium** (Orange) - 20 points per challenge
  - **Hard** (Red) - 30 points per challenge
- Tap "Start Game" to begin

### 2. Game Screen
During gameplay:
- The current player's turn is highlighted
- All players and their scores are visible at the top
- A challenge card is displayed with the challenge text
- The current player attempts the challenge
- Tap "✓ Success" if completed (adds points)
- Tap "✗ Failed" if not completed (subtracts points)
- The game automatically moves to the next player
- Round number increases after all players have had a turn
- Tap "End Game" at any time to finish

### 3. Results Screen
After ending the game:
- Winner is displayed with a trophy
- All players are ranked by score
- Final scores are shown
- Tap "Play Again" to return to setup

## Game Rules

1. **Scoring**
   - Successfully complete a challenge: +points
   - Fail a challenge: -points
   - Points depend on difficulty (10, 20, or 30)

2. **Turn Order**
   - Players take turns in order (Player 1, 2, 3, etc.)
   - After the last player, it cycles back to Player 1
   - Each complete cycle is one round

3. **Winning**
   - The game can be ended at any time
   - Player with the highest score wins
   - Scores can be negative!

## Tips

- Choose Easy mode for younger players or casual play
- Medium mode provides a good balance of challenge
- Hard mode is best for competitive players
- Challenges are random - you might get the same one twice!
- Players should be honest about completing challenges
- Have fun and be creative with the challenges!

**Easy:**

- "Do 5 jumping jacks"
- "Tell a joke"

**Medium:**

**Hard:**
